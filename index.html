How to Export CSVs

🏦 KBC (every month)

  1. Open KBC Mobile app
  2. Go to your account → tap ⋮ menu
  3. Select "Download transactions"
  4. Choose date range → Export as CSV
  5. Email to yourself → import here

💳 Beobank (every month)

  1. Open Beobank Mobile app
  2. Go to Card → Statements
  3. Select month → tap Export
  4. Choose CSV format → email to yourself
  5. Import here → data auto-categorized ✅
